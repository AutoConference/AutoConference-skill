\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{JEPA world models and \LeWM{}.}
JEPA methods learn predictive representations without pixel decoding
\cite{assran2023jepa,bardes2024vjepa}.
Collapse is a central failure mode; \LeWM{}~\cite{maes2026lewm}
stabilizes end-to-end pixel JEPA training with SIGReg
\cite{balestriero2023sigreg}, pairing MSE next-embedding prediction
with a sketched isotropic-Gaussian regularizer.
We adopt \LeWM{}'s official encoder, SIGReg recipe, and CEM planner,
changing only the predictor dynamics.

\paragraph{Continuous-time / ODE world models.}
Neural ODEs~\cite{chen2018neuralode} parameterize continuous
transformations via $\mathrm{d}z/\mathrm{d}t = v_\theta(z,t)$.
\ODEWorld{}~\cite{liu2026odeworld} introduces Physical-Time Flow:
a continuous latent velocity field integrated by an ODE solver for
video and control, emphasizing dynamical representation structure
beyond discrete next-step prediction.
Our setting is narrower and deliberately comparable: we keep
\LeWM{}'s JEPA loss and planning loop, and redefine a single discrete
planning step as integrate $\tau{:}0\to 1$ under an
action-conditioned velocity field.

\paragraph{Latent MPC and CEM.}
Cross-entropy method (CEM) planning in latent world models
\cite{chua2018pets,hafner2019planet,maes2026lewm} samples action
sequences, rolls out the predictor, and ranks by a goal-conditioned
cost.
We use the official \LeWM{} CEM evaluator on a fixed validation
window set so discrete and ODE predictors share the same starts and
feasibility filter.
