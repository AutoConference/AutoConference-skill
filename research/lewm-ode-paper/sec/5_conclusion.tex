\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method{}, a visual world model with continuous latent
dynamics: an action-conditioned ODE predictor whose one-step map is RK4
integration from $\tau{=}0$ to $\tau{=}1$, with encoder, SIGReg,
projectors, and CEM held fixed against a discrete AR baseline.
On official PushT data with a fixed 1k-window split (seed 3072;
901/99), \method{} matches discrete AR CEM success (5/93) while
reducing train and validation loss (0.234/0.506 vs.\ 0.248/0.536).

The result supports continuous latent dynamics as a viable predictor
inductive bias for planning JEPA models, but---honestly---does not
yet improve CEM under the 1k regime, nor close the gap to
full-data PushT performance reported in prior work ($\approx$96\%).
The tied CEM outcome, despite a clear loss improvement, is itself a
finding: under this budget, next-embedding fit is not a sufficient
proxy for planning success.
We view the study as a controlled comparison that isolates predictor
form; scaling data and environments is the clear next measurement.

Natural next steps are full-dataset training, multi-environment CEM,
solver ablations, and richer continuous-time formulations in the
spirit of \PriorODEWorld{}~\cite{liu2026odeworld}.
All numbers in this paper come from documented runs on the official
Hugging Face PushT release; we did not invent metrics or synthetic
episodes.
